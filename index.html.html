The College Deans

College Dean

Dr. Wenda D. Panes

Dean, College of Informatics and Computing Innovations (CICI)

Dr. Wenda D. Panes is a distinguished expert in IT and educational leadership. As the Dean of CICI, she champions digital transformation and curricula innovation. Her reputation as a certified regional quality assurance expert ensures that the college remains a leader in technology education.
College Dean

Dr. Janice H. Ching

Dean, College of Business, Management, and Sustainable Development (CBMSD)

Dr. Janice H. Ching is a business scholar and educational leader serving as the OIC Campus Dean of CBMSD. She oversees academic administration for hospitality and management programs while spearheading localized research, community extensions, and international student internships.
College Dean

Dr. Noli L. Gerona

Dean, College of Agriculture (COAG)

Dr. Noli L. Gerona is an agricultural scientist and academic administrator. In his role, he leads the development of localized farming innovation and strategic partnerships with agencies like the DSWD to advance food security and biological farming controls on campus.
College Dean

Dr. Rene T. Estomo

Dean, College of Education (COED)

Dr. Rene T. Estomo is an educational specialist managing the training, curricula, and professional deployment of pre-service teachers. He works alongside university-wide teams to direct foundational instruction and formative assessments to meet high-level institutional accreditation parameters.
